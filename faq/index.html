Frequently Asked Questions

Every question we hear most often from people considering or already using TruWave TV, grouped by topic. If you don't see yours here, message us directly on WhatsApp.

General

What is an IPTV service?

An IPTV service (also called an IPTV subscription) gives you access to live TV channels delivered over the internet instead of cable or satellite, using an app on a device like a Firestick, Smart TV, or phone. See our full guide to what IPTV is.

Is TruWave TV licensed?

Yes. TruWave TV is a licensed IPTV provider. We focus on a curated lineup of channels we're actually licensed to distribute, rather than reselling unauthorized global content.

What's the difference between live TV and the Movies & TV Shows section?

Live channels are continuous broadcast streams, the same program airs for everyone at the same time, like regular TV. Movies & TV Shows is on-demand, you pick a title and watch it whenever you want, similar to a streaming library.

Which channel categories are included?

Our lineup covers Sports, News, Kids, and Local channels. The exact channel count changes as we add newly licensed channels, so we don't quote a fixed number.

Legality & Safety

Is IPTV legal?

IPTV as a technology is completely legal, it's simply a way of delivering TV over the internet, the same method used by Hulu + Live TV or YouTube TV. What matters is whether the specific provider is actually licensed to distribute the channels it's selling. TruWave TV is a licensed provider for this reason.

How can I tell if an IPTV provider is a scam?

Watch for providers that only accept crypto payment, demand payment before any setup or test, use countdown-timer pressure tactics, or have suspiciously uniform five-star reviews posted in clusters. See our full guide to IPTV scam red flags.

Is my payment and personal information safe?

Yes. Payment is only collected through secure methods after your stream is confirmed working, and we don't sell your personal information to third parties or share your contact details with advertisers.

Do I need a VPN for IPTV?

No, not with a licensed service like TruWave TV, there's nothing to hide. The only real reason to use one is if your ISP is specifically throttling streaming traffic. See our full guide on VPNs and IPTV.

Troubleshooting

Why does my IPTV keep buffering?

Buffering is almost always your internet connection, Wi-Fi signal, or device, not your subscription. A wired connection, restarting the app, and checking for network congestion during peak hours fix most cases. See our complete buffering fix guide.

Why is my program guide (EPG) empty or not showing?

An empty guide is almost always a sync or cache issue, not a sign your channels are broken, channels can still play fine with no guide data. Refreshing the playlist and EPG separately in your app's settings fixes most cases. See our complete EPG troubleshooting guide.

What if a channel stops working or goes offline?

Message us on WhatsApp with the channel name. Most issues are resolved quickly, and if a channel is down on our end, we'll let you know and work on restoring it.

Can I request a channel that's missing from the lineup?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp. We're continually adding newly licensed channels, and customer requests help us prioritize what to add next.

What happens if my internet drops while I'm watching?

Streaming simply pauses or buffers until your connection is back, the same as any other internet streaming service. Nothing happens to your subscription or account.

How do I get support if something isn't working?

Message us on WhatsApp. Our support team is available 24/7 and responds directly, not through an automated ticket queue.
