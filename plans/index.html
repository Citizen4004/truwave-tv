How buying works

Unlike a typical checkout page, you don't pay first and hope it works. The process is:

  1. Pick a plan above, 1, 3, 6, or 12 months, and your device count.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp. A real support agent replies to set things up.
  3. We install and test your stream on your actual device using Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist.
  4. You pay only once it's confirmed working, through a secure payment method.

Never used IPTV before, or not sure which plan to pick? You can also start a free 24-hour trial first, no payment, no commitment, to see the channels and quality before choosing a term.

Frequently asked questions

How does per-month pricing work on the longer plans?

Every TruWave TV plan is billed once for the full term, not monthly. The longer the term, the lower your effective cost per month: 1 month works out to $16.99/mo, 3 months to about $11.66/mo, 6 months to about $9.17/mo, and 12 months to about $6.67/mo, over 60% less per month than paying monthly.

Is there a contract or will I be auto-billed?

No. Each plan is a single one-time payment for the term you choose (billed once, not a recurring subscription). You can cancel at any time, and your access simply continues until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do all plans include the same channels and features?

Yes. Every TruWave TV plan, 1, 3, 6, or 12 months, includes the exact same live channels, Movies & TV Shows, 4K/Full HD quality, AntiFreeze Technology, and TV Guide (EPG). The only differences between plans are the term length and price.

How much does it cost to add more devices?

Every plan supports 1 to 5 simultaneous devices, selectable at checkout, and the per-device rate gets better the more you add. Need more than 5? Message us on WhatsApp for a custom quote.

Do I have to pay before my stream is set up and tested?

No. You message us on WhatsApp after picking a plan, we set up and test your stream on your device first, and you only complete payment once you've confirmed it's working.

Can I upgrade or switch to a longer plan later?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp before your current term ends and we'll move you to a longer plan, applying the remaining time on your current plan toward the cost.

Can I get a refund?

Because you test your stream before paying, subscriptions are generally non-refundable once payment is made and the service is confirmed working. If you run into a significant, ongoing issue that isn't caused by your device or internet, message us on WhatsApp and we'll work with you on a fix. See our full refund policy for details.
