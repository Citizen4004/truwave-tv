## Notes
- Don't mass-send the same email to both — personalize the opening line once you know who you're emailing.
- If TruWave TV doesn't currently offer trial/test access for reviewers, decide that before sending — it's the single biggest lever for getting a response in this niche.
- Track replies; if one goes cold after ~10 days, one polite follow-up is normal, a second usually isn't worth it.
