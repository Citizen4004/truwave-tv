Streaming boxes & sticks

Firestick and Android TV boxes are the most common way people watch TruWave TV. Both run full Android-based apps, so they support the widest range of IPTV players, TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, or IBO Player all work well, with TiviMate's remote-friendly program guide being a favorite for living-room viewing. See how to install IPTV on Firestick for the exact steps.

Smart TVs

Most Samsung and LG Smart TVs can run an IPTV app directly, no extra box required, with IBO Player being a solid native option for both. If your TV brand isn't Samsung or LG, or doesn't support sideloading an app, a Firestick or small Android TV box plugged into an HDMI port gets you the same result with more app choice.

Mobile & tablet

iPhone and iPad run GSE Smart IPTV or IPTV Smarters Pro well, both available directly from the App Store. Android phones and tablets have the widest choice, including TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro. Useful for watching on the go, or casting to a TV when you're traveling.

Computer (PC / Mac)

IPTV Smarters Pro runs on Windows and Mac, and your subscription's M3U playlist also opens directly in VLC Media Player if you'd rather skip installing a dedicated app. See setting up IPTV on PC/Mac.

Roku, MAG boxes & Formuler

Roku doesn't allow generic IPTV player apps on its app store, but TruWave TV still works on it, see watching IPTV without an app store for how. MAG boxes and Formuler devices use a portal-based setup instead of a typical app; we provide the portal URL and your login details and walk you through it on WhatsApp.

Which device should you choose?

If you're starting from scratch and buying new hardware, a Firestick is usually the easiest recommendation, it's inexpensive, plugs into any TV's HDMI port, and supports every major player app. If you already own a Smart TV, phone, or computer, there's no need to buy anything, just install a free player app on what you have. Not sure? Message us on WhatsApp and tell us what you own, we'll recommend the simplest option for your exact setup, free, before you pay for anything.

Internet speed per device

As a guideline, aim for at least 10-15 Mbps for Full HD and 25+ Mbps for 4K, per active stream, and a wired Ethernet connection rather than Wi-Fi where possible. If you're running multiple devices on the same plan at once, add those numbers up to estimate your total bandwidth need. See our full buffering troubleshooting guide for more detail.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific box or device to use TruWave TV?

No. TruWave TV isn't tied to one piece of hardware. It works on devices you likely already own, Firestick, a Smart TV, a phone, a computer, through a free player app. You don't need to buy an IPTV box.

Can I switch which device I use?

Yes, at any time and at no extra cost. Your subscription isn't locked to a device, so you can move it to a new TV, phone, or box whenever you want.

How many devices can I use at the same time?

Every plan supports 1 to 5 simultaneous devices, selectable on the pricing page, and we support more than 5 on request with a custom quote. See how multi-device pricing works for the full breakdown.

What internet speed do I need per device?

As a guideline, aim for at least 10-15 Mbps for Full HD and 25+ Mbps for 4K, per active stream. If several devices are streaming at once, add those numbers up for your total required bandwidth.

My device isn't on this list, will it still work?

Most likely yes. TruWave TV delivers access as Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist, a standard format supported by dozens of player apps, so almost any device that can install an app or sideload one works. Message us on WhatsApp with your device and we'll confirm before you pay.

Not sure if your device is supported?

Tell us what you have on WhatsApp. We'll confirm it works and help you set it up, free, before you pay.
