How to Set Up IPTV on GSE Smart IPTV (Step-by-Step)

GSE Smart IPTV is a go-to choice for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV owners. Here's how to connect your subscription in a few steps.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install GSE Smart IPTV. Download it from the App Store (iOS/Apple TV) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Open Remote Playlists. In the app's menu, go to Remote Playlists and tap the "+" icon.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U. Select Xtream-Codes API or M3U URL based on your subscription type.
  4. Enter your details. Add your server/username/password or your M3U link, then save.
  5. Watch. Select the saved playlist from Remote Playlists to load your channels.
