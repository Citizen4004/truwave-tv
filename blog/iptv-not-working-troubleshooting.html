IPTV Not Working? Start Here

"IPTV isn't working" covers a lot of different problems, and the fix depends entirely on which one you actually have. Instead of guessing, match what you're seeing below to the right section, and go straight to the specific fix guide. Most of these take under five minutes.

Start with this 60-second check

Before digging into anything specific, rule out the most common cause first: your internet connection, not your subscription. Run a quick speed test on the same device you're having trouble on. If it's far below your normal speed, or other apps and sites are also sluggish, the problem is your connection, not IPTV. Restart your router (unplug for 30 seconds, plug back in, wait for it to fully reconnect), then restart the IPTV app itself. That combination alone resolves a surprising share of "it just stopped working" reports.

Picture freezes, stutters, or buffers constantly

If the channel loads but the picture keeps freezing, pixelating, or buffering, that's almost always a bandwidth or network-stability issue rather than anything wrong with your subscription. This is common enough that it has its own dedicated breakdown of causes and fixes: see our complete buffering fix guide for the full checklist, including the specific router and device settings that tend to cause it.

Channel list is empty, won't load, or shows "no channels"

This usually isn't a dead subscription, it's much more often a cache, login, or app-version issue. TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro specifically have a few known quirks that cause exactly this symptom. See TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro not loading channels for the step-by-step fix for both apps.

The app says "login error" or "invalid credentials"

This is a different problem from a blank channel list, and it has a different fix. It usually means either a typo in your login details, a credential that expired or was reset, or a server-side hiccup on the provider's end. See IPTV login error or invalid credentials for how to tell which one you're dealing with.

The program guide (EPG) is blank, outdated, or showing the wrong times

If channels play fine but the guide grid is empty or clearly wrong, that's a separate, much smaller issue: almost always a sync or cache problem with the guide data specifically, not your actual channels. See IPTV program guide not showing for the fix, including the TiviMate- and Smarters-specific steps.

It was working yesterday, and nothing changed on your end

When nothing you can point to changed, the most likely causes sit outside your control, and most resolve themselves without you doing anything. A provider's server can have a brief outage or maintenance window, usually measured in minutes to a couple of hours. An app update pushed automatically overnight can quietly reset a setting (this is a known pattern with MAG and Formuler boxes specifically, see our PC, Mac, MAG & Formuler setup guide if that's your device). Your ISP can also throttle or briefly disrupt the kind of traffic IPTV streams use, especially during peak evening hours, even though your general internet "works fine" for browsing. Give it 15 to 20 minutes and try again before assuming anything is actually broken.

It's specifically wifi, or specifically one device

If IPTV works fine over a wired ethernet connection but not over wifi, or works on your phone but not your Firestick, the pattern itself tells you where to look. A wifi-specific failure points to your router: try switching the device to the 5GHz band if it's currently on 2.4GHz (or vice versa, older devices sometimes do better on 2.4GHz), move the device or router closer together, and check whether a VPN is active, since some VPN configurations interfere with streaming specifically. See do you need a VPN for IPTV if you're running one and suspect it's the cause. A device-specific failure, where other devices on the same network work fine, points to that device's app: clear its cache, confirm it's updated to the latest version, and if it's a Firestick or Android box specifically, check that it has enough free storage, a nearly full device causes exactly this kind of flaky app behavior.

Tried the matching fix above and it's still not working? Message us on WhatsApp with your device, your app, and what you've already tried. A specific report gets a specific answer, a generic "it's not working" doesn't give support anything to go on.

When it's time to just contact support

Self-troubleshooting is worth it for the common, fast-to-fix issues above, but there's a point where it's more efficient to just ask. If the problem is isolated to one specific channel or a small handful while everything else plays fine, that's usually a provider-side feed issue, not something you can fix on your end. And if you've tried the restart-and-cache-clear basics and nothing's changed after 15 minutes, further guessing rarely helps; a support agent who can check the account and server side directly will get you further, faster.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my IPTV suddenly stop working with no warning?

The three most common causes, in order of likelihood: a temporary ISP or routing issue on your internet connection, a provider-side server hiccup that resolves on its own within minutes to a few hours, or an app update that reset a setting. Restarting your router and the app is worth trying before assuming anything is seriously wrong.

Is it my internet or my IPTV subscription?

Run a speed test on the same device. If your download speed is well below what it normally is, or other apps and sites are also slow, it's your connection. If internet speed looks normal but IPTV specifically won't load, it's more likely the app, the subscription, or the provider's server.

Does IPTV not working on wifi but fine on ethernet mean my subscription is broken?

No. That pattern almost always points to your wifi network, not your subscription, since a wired connection bypasses wifi entirely and still works. Check your router's band (2.4GHz vs 5GHz), signal strength at the device, and whether a VPN is active.

Why does IPTV work on my phone but not my TV app?

This usually means the subscription itself is fine, since one app on one device is working, and the issue is local to the TV app: an outdated app version, a corrupted cache, or that app's specific login/EPG settings.

When should I actually contact support instead of troubleshooting myself?

If the problem is isolated to one specific channel or a handful of channels while everything else works, or if a restart and cache clear haven't changed anything after 10 to 15 minutes, that's the point to message support with your exact device, app, and what you've already tried.
