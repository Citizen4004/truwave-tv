How to Set Up IPTV on SmartOne (Step-by-Step)

SmartOne supports two different ways to connect a subscription: a standard login screen, or a MAC-address activation some providers use instead. Here's how to set up the version that works with TruWave TV.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install SmartOne. Download the app on your device.
  2. Open the login screen. Select the option to sign in with a username and password, or add a playlist.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U. Match whichever delivery method your subscription uses.
  4. Enter your details and save. Type your credentials exactly as given, then save.
  5. Browse your channels. Your live channels and guide load on the home screen once synced.

Two ways SmartOne connects to a subscription

This is the part that trips people up when they search for SmartOne setup help: some guides describe typing in a username and password, and others describe reading a MAC address off the app and registering it on a separate website. Both are real, they're just for different kinds of providers. TruWave TV delivers subscriptions as Xtream Codes or an M3U link, so you'll use the direct login screen covered above, not the MAC-address method. That's also the quicker path: direct login starts streaming as soon as you save your details, while MAC-address activation means registering your device with the provider first and waiting on that registration before channels unlock.

If you're not sure which version you have

Open the app and look at the first screen. If it asks for a server address, username, and password (or a single playlist URL), that's the direct login, and the steps above apply as written. If it only shows a MAC address with no fields to type into, that's the portal-based version, and you'd register that MAC address with your provider instead. TruWave TV subscribers should always see the direct login screen.

Frequently asked questions

Does SmartOne need a MAC address to work?

Not always. SmartOne supports two setup methods: a direct Xtream Codes or M3U login inside the app, and a MAC-address-based activation some providers use instead. With TruWave TV you'll use the direct login, since that's how our subscriptions are delivered, and it gets you watching right away instead of waiting on a device registration.

Is SmartOne available on iOS?

Yes, SmartOne has an iOS app listed on the App Store. Check the current listing for supported devices before installing.

Can I use SmartOne on a computer?

Some versions of SmartOne note they aren't verified for macOS. If you want to watch on a computer, check the app's current store listing first, or use a player app with confirmed desktop support instead.

What's the difference between the two SmartOne setup methods?

The direct login (Xtream Codes or M3U) is entered straight into the app, the same as most player apps. The MAC-address method instead reads a code shown in the app and registers it through a separate provider portal. They're two different ways of connecting the same app to a subscription, not two different apps.
