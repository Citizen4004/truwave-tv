How to Set Up IPTV on TiviMate (Step-by-Step)

Adding an IPTV subscription to TiviMate takes about two minutes: install the app, choose Add Playlist, enter your Xtream Codes or M3U details, and let it sync. Here's the full walkthrough.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install TiviMate. On Firestick, search the Amazon Appstore for "TiviMate" and install it. On Android or Android TV, use Google Play.
  2. Open the playlist setup screen. On first launch, TiviMate prompts you to add a playlist. If you've used it before, go to Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist.
  3. Choose your delivery method. Select Xtream Codes Login if you have a server URL, username, and password, or M3U Playlist if you have a single link.
  4. Enter your credentials. Type them exactly as provided. Small typos are the most common setup issue.
  5. Let it sync. TiviMate downloads your channel list and program guide. Once done, browse channels from the main guide screen.

What TiviMate Premium actually adds

Beyond the vague "extras" label, Premium specifically unlocks: multi-screen viewing (watching more than one channel at once), a parental lock with PIN protection (this one's free in some other apps but gated behind Premium here), custom channel logos and category colors, and settings backup to the cloud so switching devices doesn't mean rebuilding your whole setup. The free tier covers full live playback, program guide, and basic favorites, genuinely enough for most people, Premium is worth it mainly if multi-screen or parental lock specifically matters to you.

Setting up favorites and multiple profiles

Long-press any channel in the guide and select "Add to Favorites" to build a shortlist accessible from its own tab, handy once your channel list grows past what's comfortable to scroll through. Separately, TiviMate supports multiple profiles under one installation (Settings → Profiles), useful if different household members want their own favorites and channel order without affecting each other, they all still use the same underlying playlist and subscription.

Remote control shortcuts worth knowing

A few shortcuts that make daily use faster: pressing a number key jumps directly to that channel number if you know it, and pressing and holding on a channel in the guide brings up a quick info/options menu instead of needing to navigate into a separate screen. While browsing the guide, TiviMate also keeps the currently playing channel visible in a small preview window rather than cutting to a static list, so you don't lose sight of what's on while looking ahead at the schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Does TiviMate work with Xtream Codes?

Yes. TiviMate supports both Xtream Codes logins and M3U playlist URLs when adding a new playlist.

Is TiviMate free?

The free tier covers full live playback, program guide, and basic favorites. Premium adds multi-screen viewing, a PIN-protected parental lock, custom channel logos, and cloud settings backup.

My TiviMate guide (EPG) is empty, why?

This usually means the EPG source needs to sync again, or the provider's guide data hasn't loaded yet. Try refreshing the playlist in Settings → Playlists, or contact your provider's support. See our full EPG troubleshooting guide for the complete fix, including TiviMate-specific steps.

Can different family members have their own favorites in TiviMate?

Yes, TiviMate supports multiple profiles under one installation (Settings → Profiles), each with its own favorites and channel order, while sharing the same underlying playlist and subscription.

Can I jump straight to a channel number in TiviMate?

Yes, pressing a number key on your remote jumps directly to that channel if you know its number, faster than scrolling through the full guide.
