TiviMate vs IPTV Smarters Pro: Which Player App Should You Use?

TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro are the two most widely used IPTV player apps. Both work with Xtream Codes and M3U playlists, both run on Firestick and Android — the real differences come down to interface, guide layout, and which platforms you want to use.

TiviMate: built for the big screen

TiviMate's biggest strength is its program guide — a dense, scrollable grid that feels closer to a cable box than a typical app menu. It's remote-navigation friendly, which makes it a favorite for Firestick and Android TV box setups where you're sitting across the room, not holding a phone.

IPTV Smarters Pro: simpler, and cross-platform

IPTV Smarters Pro trades some of that guide depth for broader platform support — it's one of the few major player apps available on iOS as well as Android, Fire TV, and Windows. If you want the same app experience on your phone and your TV, this is usually the easier pick.

Which should you choose?

  • Mostly watching on a TV with a remote? TiviMate's guide layout is hard to beat.
  • Want the same app on your phone, tablet, and TV? IPTV Smarters Pro covers more platforms.
  • Not sure? Both are free to try — install either one and add your credentials to see which interface you prefer.

Setting either one up

Both apps connect to your IPTV subscription the same way: you enter either an Xtream Codes login (server URL, username, password) or an M3U playlist URL. If you're not sure which to use, see our guide on Xtream Codes vs M3U playlists.

TruWave TV supports both delivery methods, so whichever app you already use — or decide to use — our support team will help you get it connected on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use either app with any IPTV subscription?

Yes, if your subscription is delivered as Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist, it will work in TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, or most other standard player apps.

Is TiviMate free?

TiviMate has a free tier with limited features and a paid premium tier (one-time or subscription) that unlocks multi-screen, catch-up TV, and other extras.

Which app is better for a Firestick?

Both run well on Firestick. TiviMate's remote-friendly grid guide is often preferred for TV-first use; IPTV Smarters Pro is a solid, simpler alternative, especially if you also want to use the same app on a phone.
