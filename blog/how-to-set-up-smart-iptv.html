How to Set Up IPTV on Smart IPTV (Step-by-Step)

Smart IPTV works differently from most player apps: instead of entering a username and password on your TV, you register your TV's MAC address on a separate web portal and attach your playlist there. It sounds more complicated than it is.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install Smart IPTV. Find it on your Samsung or LG TV's own app store, or Google Play if you're on Android.
  2. Find your device's MAC address. Open the app. It displays a unique MAC address and portal URL right on screen.
  3. Register on the Smart IPTV portal. On a computer or phone, go to the Smart IPTV website, create an account, and add your TV's MAC address.
  4. Upload or link your playlist. In the portal, attach your M3U playlist URL to the account tied to that MAC address.
  5. Reopen the app on your TV. Your channels load automatically once the portal syncs, no login screen needed on the TV itself.

Why it uses a MAC address instead of a login

This is the single biggest difference between Smart IPTV and apps like TiviMate or IBO Player, and it confuses people the first time. Your TV's MAC address, a unique hardware identifier, stands in for a username and password. You never type credentials into the TV app itself, instead you register that MAC address on the Smart IPTV portal website and attach your playlist there. Once it's linked, the TV app just pulls whatever playlist is tied to its MAC address automatically.

Setting up your program guide

The EPG is handled through the same portal, not the TV app. After your playlist is linked to your MAC address, check the portal for an EPG or XMLTV URL field. If your provider's playlist already includes guide data, this sometimes fills in automatically, otherwise add the XMLTV link yourself the same way you added the playlist.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Smart IPTV use a MAC address instead of a username and password?

Smart IPTV ties your subscription to your specific TV's hardware MAC address rather than login credentials. You register that MAC address on the Smart IPTV portal and attach your playlist to it there, instead of entering details directly in the TV app.

Does Smart IPTV work on devices other than Samsung and LG TVs?

It's built primarily for Samsung and LG Smart TVs, where it runs natively, but versions are also available for Android and Amazon Fire TV.

I changed my playlist URL. Do I need a new MAC address?

No. Update the playlist attached to your existing MAC address in the Smart IPTV portal, then reopen the app on your TV to sync the change. No need to re-register the device.

Can I use Smart IPTV on more than one TV?

Each TV has its own unique MAC address, so each one needs to be registered on the portal separately, even if they're using the same underlying subscription playlist.
