How to Set Up IPTV on VU Player (Step-by-Step)

VU Player runs on Smart TV, Android, iOS, and Windows, which makes it one of the more flexible player apps for mixed households. Setup follows the usual pattern: install, add your subscription, watch.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install VU Player. Find VU Player Pro for your Smart TV, Android, iOS, or Windows device.
  2. Open the add-playlist screen. Select the option to add a new playlist or login on first launch.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U. Match whichever delivery method your subscription uses.
  4. Enter your details and save. Type your credentials exactly as given, then save.
  5. Browse your channels. Your live channels and guide load on the home screen once synced.

Skip the MAC address and Device Key with TruWave TV

Some providers activate VU Player through a portal using the MAC address and Device Key shown on screen, the same kind of portal system covered in our Smart IPTV setup guide, instead of a username and password. TruWave TV delivers subscriptions as Xtream Codes or an M3U link, so you'll use the standard login screen from the steps above instead and can ignore both values entirely.

Setting up your program guide in VU Player

VU Player pulls your channel guide automatically once you've added your TruWave TV subscription using the steps above, no separate portal activation needed the way a MAC-address app requires. Open the guide view after your playlist loads and the schedule should populate within a few minutes. If it stays empty, double-check the Xtream Codes details or M3U link you entered, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the EPG is set up correctly before you pay anything.

Why VU Player is a good fit across devices

Most player apps pick a lane, Android-only, iOS-only, or Smart TV-only. VU Player covers Smart TV, Android, iOS, and Windows from one app, which matters if your household mixes device types and you'd rather not learn a different interface for the bedroom TV and the living room box. It also supports an EPG alongside M3U and Xtream Codes, so the channel guide works the same way it does in more established apps.

Frequently asked questions

What are the MAC address and Device Key in VU Player for?

Some providers activate VU Player through a portal using the MAC address and Device Key shown in the app, the same kind of portal system covered in our Smart IPTV setup guide, instead of a username and password. TruWave TV subscriptions use a direct Xtream Codes or M3U login instead, so you can ignore both values entirely.

What platforms does VU Player run on?

VU Player Pro is available on Smart TV, Android, iOS, and Windows.

Does VU Player support a program guide?

Yes, VU Player pulls your channel guide automatically from your Xtream Codes login or your M3U playlist's EPG link, with no separate activation step.

Can I use my TruWave TV subscription in VU Player on more than one device?

Yes. Add the same Xtream Codes or M3U login on every device running VU Player, Smart TV, Android, iOS, or Windows. How many can stream at once depends on the device count on your TruWave TV plan, not on VU Player itself.
