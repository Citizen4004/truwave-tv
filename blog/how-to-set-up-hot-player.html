How to Set Up IPTV on Hot Player (Step-by-Step)

Hot Player is a lesser-known pick next to TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro, but it runs through the same basic flow: install it, add your subscription, and watch. Here's exactly how.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install Hot Player. Find it on Google Play or the Amazon Appstore for Android or Fire TV.
  2. Open the add-source screen. Launch the app and select the option to add a new playlist or login.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U. Match whichever delivery method your subscription uses.
  4. Enter your details and save. Type your credentials exactly as given, then save.
  5. Browse your channels. Your live channels and guide load on the home screen once synced.

Xtream Codes or M3U: which to pick in Hot Player

If your subscription gives you a choice, Xtream Codes is usually the better fit here. It keeps Movies & TV Shows sorted into proper categories instead of dumping everything into one long list, which is what tends to happen with a plain M3U link. Live channels work fine either way, so M3U is a perfectly good fallback if that's all your provider supports.

Hot Player only runs on Android and Fire TV

Hot Player has no iOS version. It installs through Google Play or the Amazon Appstore, and it only runs on Android phones, Android tablets, and Fire TV. That's fine if your whole household watches on Android or Fire TV devices, but if anyone in your home watches on an iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV, Hot Player won't work there, and you'll need to set up a separate app on that device instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hot Player available on iOS?

No. Hot Player is built for Android and Fire TV only, through Google Play and the Amazon Appstore. There's no version for iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV.

Should I use Xtream Codes or M3U in Hot Player?

Use whichever your subscription provides, but Xtream Codes generally keeps Movies & TV Shows better organized into categories than a raw M3U link does. If your provider offers both, Xtream Codes is usually the smoother option.

Why does Hot Player's version or features seem different from what I've read elsewhere?

Smaller apps like Hot Player update and change more often than the big-name players, and older guides can go stale quickly. Check the app's current store listing for the latest version and feature set.

Can I switch from Hot Player to a different app later?

Yes. Your subscription credentials aren't tied to Hot Player specifically, so you can add them to another player app at any time.
