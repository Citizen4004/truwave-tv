Best Device to Watch NHL Games

The right device for watching NHL games on IPTV depends more on what you already own than any single "best" answer. Here's a practical breakdown of the main options, what actually matters across the regular season and playoffs, and how to get set up on whichever one you pick.

What actually matters in a streaming device for sports

For live sports specifically, the things that matter most aren't flashy specs, they're reliability and connection quality. A device that runs a streaming app smoothly and sits close enough to your router (or is wired directly to it) will handle NHL games better than a more "powerful" device on a weak Wi-Fi signal across the house. Beyond that, it comes down to what fits your existing setup and budget.

Firestick

Amazon's Fire TV Stick is one of the most common starting points for IPTV, mainly because it's inexpensive, plugs directly into any TV with an HDMI port, and is simple to set up. It supports the major player apps used for IPTV subscriptions. For most people building a streaming setup from scratch specifically to watch NHL games, this is the lowest-friction option.

Android TV boxes

A dedicated Android TV box offers more processing headroom than a Firestick in some cases, and often supports wired Ethernet more easily, which can help with stream stability during high-traffic viewing windows like playoff nights. It's a reasonable step up if you want a more dedicated streaming device rather than a stick that shares space with other cables.

Smart TV apps

If your TV already has a built-in app store, you may not need extra hardware at all. This is the simplest setup in terms of fewer devices to manage, though app support and update frequency can vary more by TV brand and model than with a dedicated streaming device. Check our Devices page for which smart TV platforms are currently supported.

Phones and tablets

TruWave TV's app also runs on Android and iOS phones, which is useful in two situations: watching on the go when you're away from your main TV, and as a backup if your primary setup has an issue mid-game. It's not a replacement for a TV-based setup for most people, but it's worth having installed regardless.

What to prioritize if you're buying new hardware

If you don't already own any of these and are deciding what to buy specifically for hockey season, prioritize in this order: a stable internet connection first (this matters more than the device itself), then whichever device fits your existing TV setup with the least friction, then wired Ethernet support if your router and TV's location make that realistic. A mid-range device on a strong connection will consistently outperform a high-end device on a weak one, and that gap matters most during the playoffs when everyone's streaming at once.

Setting up for a watch party

If you're hosting rather than watching alone, a single large screen with a strong connection generally beats multiple smaller devices spread across a room. If the plan is to run the game in more than one room at once, that's a simultaneous-connections question, not a device question, so check your plan's device limit ahead of time rather than discovering it mid-game. Setting up TruWave TV on your device

Setup is generally the same across devices: install the app, log in with your subscription details, and load a channel to confirm everything's working before puck drop rather than during it. We'd recommend doing this a few days ahead of a big game, especially during the playoffs, not minutes before. Frequently asked questions

What's the best device for watching NHL games on IPTV?

There isn't one universal answer. A Firestick or Android TV box is the simplest starting point for most people, since both are inexpensive and straightforward to set up, but a smart TV with a built-in app avoids needing extra hardware at all.

Can I watch NHL games on my phone with IPTV?

Yes, TruWave TV's app runs on Android and iOS phones, which is useful for watching on the go or as a backup if your main TV setup has an issue.

Do I need a powerful device to stream NHL games smoothly?

Not an especially powerful one, but a wired or strong Wi-Fi connection matters more than raw device specs for avoiding buffering, especially during the playoffs when viewership spikes.

What devices does TruWave TV work on?

TruWave TV's app runs on Firestick, Android TV boxes, Android and iOS phones, and most smart TVs. Check our Devices page for the full compatibility list before you subscribe.

Should I use a wired connection for playoff games?

If your device and router support it, yes. A wired connection is more stable than Wi-Fi for high-bitrate sports streams, especially during the playoffs, and even more so in a Game 7, when viewership spikes well above a regular-season night.

Can I switch devices without losing my subscription?

Yes, your IPTV subscription isn't tied to one device. You can install the app on a new device and log in with the same credentials.

How do I set up TruWave TV on a new device?

What's the best setup for hosting a watch party?

A single large screen with a wired or strong connection beats multiple smaller devices for a group watching together. Check your plan's simultaneous-connection limit if you're planning to run the game in more than one room at once.
