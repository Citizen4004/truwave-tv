How to Set Up IPTV on Kodi & Jellyfin (and Why Plex Is Different)

Kodi and Jellyfin both accept an IPTV M3U playlist directly, built in, no extra bridge software needed. Plex doesn't work the same way, and it's worth knowing why before you spend time trying to force it.

Kodi

Kodi handles live TV through an addon called PVR IPTV Simple Client, which comes bundled with Kodi but isn't enabled by default.

  1. Go to Settings → Add-ons → My add-ons → PVR clients and open PVR IPTV Simple Client.
  2. Enable it, then open its configuration.
  3. Under the General tab, set the M3U Play List URL to your TruWave TV playlist link.
  4. Under the EPG Settings tab, set the XMLTV URL if you have one, this fills in the program guide.
  5. Save, then restart Kodi. Go to the TV section and your channels should populate within a minute.

If channels don't appear after restarting, double-check the playlist URL was pasted in full with nothing cut off, that's the most common cause.

Jellyfin

Jellyfin has native Live TV support built into its dashboard, no addon needed.

  1. In Jellyfin's web dashboard, go to Live TV in the left menu.
  2. Under Tuner Devices, click to add a new one and choose M3U Tuner as the type.
  3. Paste your playlist URL and save.
  4. Under TV Guide Data Providers, add your XMLTV URL the same way for program guide data.
  5. Jellyfin will scan and import your channels, this can take a minute or two the first time depending on channel count.

Once set up, Live TV appears as its own section alongside your movies and shows, in the same interface.

Why Plex is a different situation

Plex's Live TV & DVR feature was built around physical over-the-air tuners (HDHomeRun devices specifically), not internet IPTV playlists. There's no official setting where you paste an M3U URL or Xtream Codes login into Plex the way you can with Kodi or Jellyfin. Some third-party tools exist that create a virtual tuner Plex can see and feed it your IPTV playlist, but that's an extra piece of software to install, configure, and keep running, on top of your subscription, and it's not something we can troubleshoot or support directly. If Plex is specifically where you want live TV, Jellyfin is the closest equivalent: same kind of unified media library feel, with IPTV support actually built in rather than bolted on.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use IPTV on Plex the same way as Kodi or Jellyfin?

Not directly. Plex's Live TV & DVR feature is built around physical HDHomeRun tuners, not internet M3U or Xtream Codes playlists. Unofficial third-party bridge tools exist that convert a playlist into something Plex can read, but they're an extra layer of setup and maintenance on top of your subscription, not something we can support directly. If Plex is your main media app, Jellyfin's Live TV works the same way Plex's library does and accepts IPTV playlists natively.

Do I need the EPG URL, or just the channel playlist?

The playlist alone gets your channels playing. The EPG (XMLTV) URL is what fills in the program guide with show names and times. Both Kodi and Jellyfin work without it, you'll just see channels with no schedule information.

Why would I use Kodi or Jellyfin instead of a dedicated IPTV app like TiviMate?

Mainly if you're already using Kodi or Jellyfin as your main media center for movies and shows, and want live TV folded into the same interface instead of switching apps. A dedicated app like TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro is usually a smoother experience if live TV is your main use case.
