How to Install IPTV on Firestick (Step-by-Step)

You can get IPTV running on an Amazon Firestick in about five minutes: enable unknown sources, install the Downloader app, use it to install a player app like IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate, then enter your subscription details. No computer required.

What you'll need

  • An Amazon Firestick connected to Wi-Fi or ethernet
  • An IPTV subscription with Xtream Codes login details or an M3U playlist URL
  • About five minutes

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Enable apps from unknown sources. On your Firestick home screen, go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options, and turn on Apps from Unknown Sources. This allows you to install apps outside the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Install the Downloader app. Back on the home screen, open the Appstore, search for "Downloader," and install the free app from AFTVnews. This is the tool you'll use to install your player app.
  3. Download your IPTV player app. Open Downloader, and enter the official download URL for your chosen player app (for example, IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate, see our comparison guide if you're not sure which to pick). Let it download and install.
  4. Enter your subscription details. Open the newly installed app. You'll be prompted to add a playlist. Enter either your Xtream Codes login (server URL, username, password) or your M3U playlist URL, both provided by your IPTV subscription.
  5. Let it sync, then watch. The app will load your channel list and program guide. Once it finishes, your live channels are ready to browse.

"There was a problem parsing the package" when installing

This specific error shows up during the Downloader install step, and it's almost always one of two things on Firestick. First, check your storage: go to Settings → My Fire TV → About → Network, scroll down to see available storage, and if it's close to full (common on the base 8GB Firestick), uninstall an app or two you don't use before retrying. Second, confirm Unknown Sources is actually still on, Firestick software updates occasionally reset this setting back off without warning, so it's worth rechecking even if you enabled it earlier in this same session.

Updating your player app later

Since you installed your IPTV app outside the Amazon Appstore, it won't auto-update the way Appstore apps do. When a new version comes out, open Downloader again and enter the exact same download URL you used the first time, it will detect the existing install and offer to update over it rather than creating a duplicate. You don't need to uninstall the old version first.

Making your IPTV app easier to reach

By default, a newly installed app gets buried at the end of your Firestick's app row. From the home screen, highlight the app icon, press and hold the Select button on your remote, and choose "Move to Front." For even faster access, some people set their player app to replace the home screen entirely (Settings → Preferences → Home Screen Default Launcher, on Fire OS versions that support it), though this is optional and reversible.

Frequently asked questions

Can I install IPTV on Firestick without a computer?

Yes. Everything happens on the Firestick itself using the Downloader app, no computer or sideloading tools needed.

Which IPTV player app should I install?

TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro are the two most popular choices and both work well on Firestick. See our comparison to decide which fits you.

What if my channels don't load?

Double-check your Xtream Codes details or M3U URL for typos, confirm your Firestick has a stable internet connection, and restart the app. If it still doesn't load, contact your provider's support team.

Why do I get "there was a problem parsing the package" when installing?

Usually low storage space or Unknown Sources getting switched off again after a Firestick update. Check your available storage under Settings → My Fire TV → About, and recheck that Unknown Sources is still enabled before retrying.

How do I update my IPTV app on Firestick?

Open Downloader again and re-enter the same download URL you used originally. It installs over the existing app as an update rather than creating a duplicate, no uninstall needed first.
