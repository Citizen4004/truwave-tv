IPTV Program Guide (EPG) Not Showing? How to Fix It

An empty or outdated program guide doesn't mean your channels are broken — it's almost always a sync or cache issue, and it's quick to fix.

What the EPG actually is

EPG stands for Electronic Program Guide — the schedule data showing what's currently airing and what's next on each channel. It's separate from the video stream itself, which is why channels can play perfectly fine even when the guide is empty.

Step-by-step fix

  1. Refresh the playlist and EPG separately. Most apps have two different refresh actions — refreshing just the channel list doesn't always pull new guide data. Check your app's settings for a dedicated "Refresh EPG" or "Update Guide" option.
  2. Check your device's date, time, and timezone. An incorrect clock can shift the whole guide by hours or make it look empty. Set these to automatic, then restart the app.
  3. Clear the app's cache. On Android TV and Firestick, clearing cache (Settings → Apps → [Player App] → Clear Cache) and restarting often rebuilds a stuck guide.
  4. Test a different channel. Not every channel carries full guide data — if most channels show a guide but a few don't, it's likely those specific channel records, not your setup.
  5. Try another player app. If the guide works in a second app with the same subscription, the issue is with the first app's settings, not your service.

Frequently asked questions

What is an EPG in IPTV?

EPG stands for Electronic Program Guide — it's the schedule data that shows what's currently playing and what's coming up next on each channel.

Why is my channel guide empty?

Usually a cache issue, an unsynced EPG source, or a channel that simply doesn't have guide data attached to it. Refreshing the playlist and EPG in your app's settings fixes most cases.

Does an empty guide mean my channels won't play?

No. The guide is just schedule information — channels can stream fine even if the program guide data is temporarily missing or outdated.
