How to Set Up IPTV on IBO Player (Step-by-Step)

IBO Player's clean interface and broad Smart TV support make it a popular pick. Setup follows the same pattern as most player apps: install, add your playlist, and watch.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install IBO Player. Find it on Google Play, the App Store, Amazon Appstore, or your Samsung/LG TV's app store.
  2. Open playlist settings. Select the option to add a new playlist or account on first launch.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U. Match whichever delivery method your subscription uses.
  4. Enter your details and save. Type your credentials exactly as given, then save.
  5. Browse your channels. Your live channels and guide load on the home screen once synced.

What IBO Player Pro actually adds over the free version

The FAQ mentions Pro has "additional features," here's what that concretely means: catch-up/replay TV for channels that support it (watching something that already aired, not just live), local recording to your device's storage, and the multi-screen view for watching more than one channel at once. If you only need standard live playback, the free version covers that fully, these extras matter mainly if catch-up or recording is something you'd actually use.

Managing multiple playlists as separate accounts

IBO Player lets you save more than one playlist as a separate "account," useful if you're testing a trial while keeping an existing subscription active, or maintaining a backup. Switch between them from the account selection screen rather than deleting and re-entering credentials each time, each account keeps its own channel list, EPG, and favorites independently.

Setting up your program guide

Like most player apps, IBO Player treats the EPG as a separate setting from the playlist itself. After adding your account, check its settings for an EPG URL field, if your provider's Xtream Codes login already includes guide data, this often populates automatically, but if your channels load with no schedule information, this is the first place to check.

Frequently asked questions

Does IBO Player work directly on Samsung and LG TVs?

Yes, IBO Player is available natively on many Samsung and LG Smart TV app stores, not just through casting from another device.

Is there a difference between IBO Player and IBO Player Pro?

IBO Player Pro is a paid version with additional features; the standard IBO Player is free and covers core live TV playback.

What does IBO Player Pro add over the free version?

Catch-up/replay TV on channels that support it, local recording to your device, and a multi-screen view for watching more than one channel at once. Standard live playback works the same on both versions.

Can I save more than one playlist in IBO Player?

Yes. IBO Player supports multiple saved accounts, each with its own channel list, EPG, and favorites. Switch between them from the account selection screen instead of re-entering credentials each time.
